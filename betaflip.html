Verdict: the flip is real as a description of the tape — leadership alternates in regimes with a median of ~3 weeks and a mean of ~1 month (occasional 4–6 month runs), ~12 flips/yr. But the regime's sign does not predict the next month's high-minus-low beta spread (hit rates ≈ coin toss; if anything mild mean-reversion). Use the angle as a nowcast of what kind of tape you're in and for hedging posture — not as a standalone timing trigger. It also co-moves with the market's own 21d return (corr 0.6–0.75): high-beta leads in up-tapes; the interesting days are when the sign disagrees with the tape.

Method & caveats

  • Betas: 252d rolling vs equal-weight composite of the same universe (returns clipped ±40% for the composite), lagged 21d so the regression never sees the return window it explains. Cross-section requires ≥30% of names.
  • Regime = sign of the 5-day median of the daily slope; unsmoothed sign flips ~2.3× more often.
  • Forward test uses top/bottom beta quintiles, equal-weight, overlapping 21d windows — treat significance informally.
  • Survivorship: today's constituents projected back ~11y; high-beta winners are over-represented, so level (not sign) of spreads is flattered.
  • Price-series rebase glitches auto-repaired before analysis: CLDN.L (x1), MEGP.L (x2), ROSE.L (x4).
